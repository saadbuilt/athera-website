    How this is scored

    Every hour for the past seven days, each model's temperature forecast for Karachi, made one, two and three days earlier, is compared with what the analysis says happened. The bar is the mean absolute error across those hours. The bias line says whether the model runs warm or cool here.

    What it isn't. The reference is Open-Meteo's best-match analysis for this grid point, not a thermometer at Jinnah airport. A model that feeds that analysis has home advantage, and one week in one season is a small sample. Read it as a guide to who to trust this week, not a verdict.
